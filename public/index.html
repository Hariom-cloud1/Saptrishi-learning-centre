Our Story

From a Small Spark to a Movement

Founded in 2023, Saptrishi Learning Centre began when a group of educators noticed a stark reality: while the city of Kanpur grew vertically, the children in the surrounding slum communities were being left behind. We realised that for these children, a school bag isn't just a kit — it's a ticket out of the cycle of poverty.

"Education is not a luxury; it is the fundamental right of every child, regardless of the pincode they are born in."

With your support, we turn that belief into bridge classrooms, meals, mentors and machines — the everyday tools a child needs to stay in school and imagine a different future.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, holistic education and life-skills training to 1,000+ children living in urban slums by 2027.

Our Vision

An India where a child's potential is limited only by their imagination, not their circumstances.
