The Guidance Gap

First-generation learners often lack academic support at home.

For many of our students, they are the first person in their family to attend school at all. That means there's no one at home who has done homework, read a textbook, or understands how the school system works — so children are often left to navigate it entirely alone.

This guidance gap shows up in small but compounding ways: not knowing how to ask for help, missing deadlines, or simply believing that continuing school "isn't for people like us."

Our teachers and mentors step into that gap, offering the kind of everyday academic support and encouragement that many children would otherwise never receive — helping them believe that they, too, belong in a classroom.
