Nutritional Support

A hungry child cannot focus. We provide regular meals and snacks for physical and mental growth.

Hunger is one of the biggest silent barriers to learning — a child who hasn't eaten cannot concentrate, retain lessons, or stay energetic through a school day.

Twice a month, our centres serve nutritious meals and snacks designed with basic dietary needs in mind, sourced and prepared in partnership with local community volunteers.

Beyond nutrition, mealtimes double as a space for children to socialize, build friendships, and feel cared for — often the first consistent, warm meal many of them get outside their own homes.
