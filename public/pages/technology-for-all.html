Technology for All

Through Chromebooks and shared devices, we make sure "slum" never means "offline."

Growing up in a slum shouldn't mean growing up offline. We provide shared Chromebooks and basic devices at our learning centres so children get hands-on exposure to typing, the internet, and digital learning tools — skills that are now essential for almost every future job.

Sessions are kept simple and supervised, focusing on safe, educational use rather than just entertainment, and are often the very first time a child has touched a computer.
