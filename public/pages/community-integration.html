Community Integration

We work closely with parents, especially mothers, so they become advocates for their children's education.

Lasting change doesn't happen in a classroom alone — it happens when the whole family believes in a child's education.

We run regular sessions with parents, especially mothers, to help them understand the long-term value of schooling over short-term income from child labour.

Mothers are also invited into our vocational training programs, so that as their children study, they too gain new skills and a sense of financial independence — turning parents from bystanders into active advocates for their children's future.
